Six ideas for the rebuild: same pocket-sized, one-screen, button-driven format as the date site, but pointed at being cute and funny instead. Every card below is a working mini-demo. Poke them.

is this website cute?
Option 1 · closest to what you have

The Rigged Poll

A dumb question with a "No" button that dodges your cursor, and a "Yes" that grows each time you miss. Ends in confetti and a self-congratulating message. Keeps your existing flow (splash → persuade → celebrate) and confetti canvas.

Pattern from pandaboi1/Valentine and Ask-out-your-Valentine

boop me
Option 2 · pure comfort

Boop the Cat

One cat. Click it. A counter ticks up, the cat gets progressively more annoyed, then unlocks absurd "achievements" ("Menace to Society" at 50). No backend: counter lives in localStorage.

Idea from the "pointless yet funny" genre in awesome-useless-websites

ask me anything
Option 3 · most replayable

Unhelpful Oracle

A magic 8-ball that gives confident, useless life advice ("Yes, but only if you drink water first"). Easy to extend: it's just an array of strings, so adding inside jokes is one line each.

Same shape as the joke generator AndrewMedvedev42/Show_Me_A_Joke_Generator (I only saw it in a search snippet, but the one it describes uses the Fetch API; ours wouldn't need any network)

please do not press the button
Option 4 · tiny and dumb

The Do-Not-Press Button

One button, escalating guilt. Each press changes the page: nervous warnings, fake error screens, the button begging you to stop, finally a tiny apology. Great as a one-evening build.

Genre reference: awesome-useless-websites

i am watching you 👀
Option 5 · lowest effort, highest charm

Googly Eyes

Giant cartoon eyes that follow your cursor (or finger on mobile). Click to make them blink; leave it idle and they fall asleep with little Zs. About 30 lines of JS.

Inspired by the cursor-follow gag from Pointer Pointer, also listed in awesome-useless-websites

ready to load your vibes?
Option 6 · best for sharing

Fake Loading Screen

A progress bar that narrates nonsense ("Untangling headphones…", "Convincing the cat…") and lands on a personalized punchline. Share-friendly because the end screen can say whatever you want.
